## What Field Service Software Does for a Small Business

Field service software (you'll also see it called home service business software or FSM software) replaces the paper, spreadsheets and texts most small crews start with. In practice it does five things:

- **Booking:** customers request work from your website or a link, and it lands in one inbox instead of your personal phone.
- **Quotes:** you send a professional quote from your phone, and the customer approves it online.
- **Scheduling:** jobs go on a shared calendar your crew can see, with reminders sent to customers the day before.
- **Invoicing and payments:** the invoice goes out when the job's done, with a pay-online link, and unpaid ones get chased automatically on most paid plans.
- **Records:** every customer's history, photos and notes in one place, so the next visit doesn't start from zero.

The payoff is time and cash flow. Most owners say the first thing they notice is getting paid faster, because invoices go out the same day instead of on Sunday night.

You probably don't need it with five customers. Somewhere around 20 regulars, or the first time you hire help, a notebook starts costing you jobs.

## How Field Service Software for Small Business Is Priced

Before the reviews, look at how each one bills. It changes which is cheapest for you.

- **Per plan with user tiers (Jobber):** You pay for a bracket of users (1, 5, 10, 15). Good if your team size sits at the top of a bracket, wasteful if it sits just above one.
- **Per plan with users included (Housecall Pro, Contractor Foreman):** A fixed price covers a set number of people. Good for crews that fill those seats.
- **Per job (ServiceM8):** Unlimited users, but you pay by how many jobs you run a month. Great for a crew that does a few big jobs, expensive for a solo window cleaner doing 15 small jobs a day.
- **Per user (Tradify):** Simple, but the bill grows with every hire.

Say you have four people doing about 120 jobs a month. Jobber Connect at the 5-user tier is $149/mo. Housecall Pro Essentials is $149/mo. ServiceM8 Growing (150 jobs, unlimited users) is $79/mo. Tradify Pro at four users is $204/mo. Same crew, very different bills.

## How to Choose Field Service Software for Your Small Business

Ask yourself three things before you start any trial:

1. **How many people will use it in a year?** If you're about to hire, price the plan for the team you'll have, not the one you have now.
2. **How many jobs a month?** High volume and small tickets point away from ServiceM8. A few big jobs point toward it.
3. **What does your bookkeeper use?** QuickBooks or Xero sync is the feature that pushes most people up a plan.

Then put three real jobs through two trials, from quote to paid. The one that felt less like work is your answer.
