# About {{site_name}}

{{site_name}} publishes plain-English software guides for small home-service businesses: house cleaners, lawn care and landscaping crews, handymen, pressure washers, HVAC, plumbing and electrical shops.

We focus on what owner-operators actually need to know before paying for a tool: the real monthly cost at their team size, the features that matter for their trade, and the trade-offs nobody mentions on the pricing page.

## How we research

- Prices and plan features come from each vendor's official pricing page, with the date we checked them.
- User experience points come from recent reviews on sites like G2, Capterra and Reddit. We only keep points that come up repeatedly.
- We update our guides when prices or plans change.
